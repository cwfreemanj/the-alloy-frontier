# Alloy Frontier: The First Forge (playtest build)

Thanks for testing! This is the first vertical slice: one region and one quest, about 20–40 minutes.
Everyone who opens the link shares the same world.

**Play:** https://alloy-frontier.onrender.com in Chrome or Edge, on a desktop or laptop (keyboard
and mouse needed).
- The free server sleeps when nobody is on, so the first load can take about a minute.
- **Sign in** (create an account on the title screen) to keep your character on the server.
  Guests are saved in this browser only.
- The build version is at the bottom of the title screen and in the Esc menu. Note it with your
  feedback.

## Controls
- **Click the game view** to capture the mouse. **Esc** opens the menu and releases it.
- **Moving:** WASD · Shift sprint · Space jump · mouse wheel zoom.
- **Fighting:**
  - Left-click / J: light attack (3-hit combo) · F: heavy · hold right-click / L: block.
  - Q: dodge · R: Guardbreaker (breaks shields) · G: Storm Arc (if your sword has it).
  - Tab: lock on to a target.
- **1–4:** combat skills (unlocked by weapon mastery). **5 / 6:** healing salve / stamina tonic.
- **E:** talk, gather, use, search or examine. **I:** character, inventory, proficiencies and
  skills. **F3:** FPS overlay.

## What to do
Follow the orange marker on the minimap and the tracker in the top right.
1. Talk to Master Smith Orla at the forge in Emberhold Village.
2. Head up the Ironwood Trail, then the Ironglass Quarry, to gather iron ore, ironwood and
   ironglass. Herbs along the trail brew into potions.
3. Check Bram's shop, then forge a sword at the anvil inside the forge: Quick Forge first, then
   a Masterwork. Orla explains each step on the forge screen.
4. Take your sword to the Dormant Foundry and defeat the Warden.

Along the way:
- search barrels, the forge loft chest and hidden trail caches;
- examine Orla's masterwork, Emberwake, on the forge wall;
- check enemies' levels above their heads.

## Feedback worth noting
- Anything confusing: where to go, or what a forge choice does.
- Fights that felt unfair, too easy, or unresponsive.
- **Performance:** after a few minutes of play, press Esc → **Copy performance report** and paste
  it into your notes. It includes your GPU, settings and frame rate.
- **Bugs:** what you did just before, the build version, and a screenshot if possible.

The forms for the M4 playthroughs (time, deaths, five questions) are in [M4.md](M4.md).

**Known rough edges:**
- Animations are retargeted Mixamo clips fitted to the combat timing, so some transitions may pop.
- The Quarry Sentinel is still a placeholder shape.
- Combat balance hasn't been tuned by real players yet.
- Pine trees look blocky up close.
