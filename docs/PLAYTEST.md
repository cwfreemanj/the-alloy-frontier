# Alloy Frontier: The First Forge (playtest build)

Thanks for testing! This is an early vertical slice: one region, one quest, about 20–40 minutes.

**Play:** open the link in Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop (keyboard and mouse needed).
Your progress saves in your browser automatically. Use the same browser to continue.

## Controls
- **Click the game view** to capture the mouse. **Esc** opens the menu and releases it.
- **WASD** move · **Shift** sprint · **Space** jump · **mouse wheel** zoom
- **Left-click / J** light attack (3-hit combo) · **F** heavy · **hold right-click / L** block
- **Q** dodge · **R** Guardbreaker (breaks shields) · **G** Storm Arc (if your sword has it)
- **Tab** lock on to a target · **E** talk / gather / use · **I** inventory · **F3** FPS overlay

## What to do
Follow the orange marker on the minimap and the tracker in the top-right.
1. Talk to Orla at the forge in Emberhold Village.
2. Head up the Ironwood Trail, then the Ironglass Quarry, to gather materials.
3. Check Bram's shop, then forge a sword at the forge: Quick Forge first, then a Masterwork.
4. Take your sword to the Dormant Foundry and defeat the Warden.

## Feedback worth noting
- Anything confusing (where to go, what a forge choice does)
- Fights that felt unfair, too easy, or unresponsive
- Low frame rate (press F3 and note the number) plus your GPU, if you know it
- Bugs: what you did just before, and a screenshot if possible

Pick **Maria** or **Exo Gray** on the title screen (other players see your choice).

Known rough edges: animations are retargeted Mixamo clips fitted to the combat timing, so some
transitions may pop; the Quarry Sentinel is still a placeholder shape; combat balance hasn't been tuned
by real players yet; health regenerates out of combat (no potions in this slice).
